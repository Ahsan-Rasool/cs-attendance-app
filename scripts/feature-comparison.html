CS Attendance Plus

What this app has that the official CS Attendance System app (v1.4.4, student side) does not

New features in CS Attendance Plus

FeatureWhat it does
Dark modeSystem / Light / Dark theme, one-tap toggle on the home screen. The official app is light-only.
Attendance tabOne screen for all courses: overall ring, present/absent counts, per-course bars against the minimum, recent absences, and a calendar for any single course.
What-if planner (per course)Every course has its own "if I miss N / if I attend N" calculator showing the resulting percentage.
"Can I skip?" guidanceShows how many lectures you can still miss, or how many you must attend in a row to get back to the minimum, on the home screen, course list and course page.
Adjustable minimum %Set your own attendance minimum (default 75%); warnings and bars follow it.
Add your own timetable classesAdd personal classes or activities with day, time and room. Stored on your phone only; never sent to the server. They appear in the timetable, today's list, PDFs and reminders.
Class reminders with roomWeekly notification 5/10/15/30 minutes before each class showing class name, start time, room and teacher. Works offline.
Timetable PDFDownload or share a day-by-day PDF of your week, including your own classes.
Attendance PDFCourse summary table with status ("can miss N" / "attend next N") plus a date-by-date lecture log, for all courses or one.
GPA calculatorPick expected grades per course to see this semester's GPA and your projected CGPA; enter a target CGPA to see the GPA you need.
DashboardGreeting, overall attendance, CGPA, courses at risk, and today's classes marked Now / Next / Done.
Attendance streakCounts how many lectures in a row you have attended.
Offline cacheShows your last-seen data when there is no connection.
Demo modeExplore the whole app with made-up data and no account.
Installs side by sideSeparate package (lms.iotpro.plus), so it can sit next to the official app.

How this was checked: the comparison is based on analysis of the official v1.4.4 app's published code, covering its student screens. Items marked "not found" mean no sign of them was found there; a newer official version may differ. CS Attendance Plus is an unofficial client, has not been tested against the live server, and GPA figures are estimates on a standard 4.0 scale. Students cannot mark attendance in either app — only teachers and TAs can.
